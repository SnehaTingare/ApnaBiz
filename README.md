<h2 id="overview">🔍 Overview</h2>

> **ApnaBiz** is an AI-powered local business discovery platform focused on **Nashik city**.  
It helps users find nearby shops and services while enabling business owners to list their businesses with contact details, categories, and map-based locations.

The platform is built using the **MERN stack** with a **React + Vite frontend**, designed to be scalable for future expansion to Maharashtra and India.

<img src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/73097560/115834477-dbab4500-a447-11eb-908a-139a6edaec5c.gif" width="100%">

<h2 id="features">✨ Features</h2>

- 🏪 **Add Your Business** – Business owners can list their shops and services  
- 🔍 **AI-Powered Search** (Planned) – Smart search using LangChain & Pinecone  
- 🗂️ **Category-Based Browsing** – Grocery, Medicals, Beauty, Agriculture & more  
- 🗺️ **Map Integration** – View exact business locations  
- 📱 **Responsive UI** – Mobile-first modern design  
- 🔐 **Authentication System** – Login & Signup for users and business owners  
- 🧩 **Scalable Architecture** – City → State → Country expansion ready
